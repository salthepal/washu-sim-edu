## Why debriefing is the centerpiece

Most of the learning in simulation happens in the debrief, not the scenario.
This module is where residents move from *running* a case to *teaching* with one.

## Advocacy-inquiry in one line

Pair an observation and your point of view (advocacy) with genuine curiosity
about the learner's reasoning (inquiry): *"I saw epinephrine was held until the
second set of vitals — I was concerned that delayed treatment. I'm curious what
you were seeing at that moment."*
