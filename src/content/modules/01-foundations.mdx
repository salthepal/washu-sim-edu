## Why this module exists

Before residents write or run cases, they need a shared mental model of what
simulation is *for*. The headline idea: fidelity is a means, not an end —
**functional task alignment** (does the sim let learners practice the actual
decision?) matters more than realism for its own sake.

## The pre-brief

A good pre-brief is where psychological safety is earned, not assumed. Cover the
fiction contract, confidentiality, the "we assume everyone is competent and
trying their best" stance, and orientation to the environment.
