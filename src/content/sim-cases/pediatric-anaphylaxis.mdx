## Scenario summary

A 7-year-old is brought in by a parent <Note kind="objective" title="Trigger is unambiguous by design" note="The food exposure is stated up front so the case never tests diagnostic breadth. With recognition made easy, the whole case can pressure-test the single objective that matters at PGY1-2: acting decisively.">minutes after eating peanut at a birthday party</Note>, with <Note kind="cue" title="Manikin cue: visible urticaria" note="Moulage and programmed skin findings give learners a concrete early sign to anchor on. The cue is deliberately obvious; the test is whether they act on it, not whether they spot it.">spreading hives and lip swelling</Note>.

On arrival the child is anxious and beginning to develop <Note kind="decision-point" title="The case is built around this moment" note="Progressive stridor is the scripted decision point: give IM epinephrine now, or wait. Every other element exists to make this choice feel consequential and time-pressured. In debrief, this is where you spend your time.">audible inspiratory stridor</Note>.

## The complication

As the team gathers, <Note kind="distractor" title="Planted anchoring trap" note="The parent's request reliably pulls inexperienced teams toward a bronchodilator-first frame. It is not there to be cruel; it is there so the debrief can name anchoring bias out loud and rehearse holding the anaphylaxis frame under social pressure.">the parent asks the team to just give a breathing treatment like last time</Note>. If the team defers epinephrine, vitals deteriorate on a fixed timeline.

The scenario runs <Note kind="fidelity" title="Fidelity matched to objective" note="High-fidelity manikin rather than a standardized patient, because the objective hinges on changing vitals and airway sounds over time, which is physiologic feedback the learner must read and respond to. The fidelity is here to serve the decision, not for its own sake.">on a high-fidelity manikin with programmed deterioration</Note>, and a <Note kind="safety" title="Psychological-safety scaffold" note="The confederate parent is scripted to stay within bounds, emotional but never abusive, so the case stays inside the fiction contract. Brief the confederate explicitly; an over-acted parent derails learning and undermines safety.">confederate plays the parent within agreed limits</Note>.
